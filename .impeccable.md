## Design Context

### Users
Researchers, prospective collaborators, recruiters, and students visiting Yongli Xiang's academic homepage to quickly understand his background, research focus, publications, and academic activities.

### Brand Personality
Warm, scholarly, and approachable. The site should feel personal and editorial while remaining concise and credible.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained, light academic homepage with serif body typography, handwritten display headings, muted warm accents, subtle paper texture, and generous but efficient spacing. Preserve the existing visual language rather than introducing a new design system.

### Design Principles
- Make research output easy to scan without flattening it into generic cards.
- Use width and spacing to improve readability while keeping prose to a comfortable line length.
- Pair each publication with a concise, plain-language statement of its core contribution.
- Preserve image aspect ratios and adapt layouts cleanly on narrow screens.
- Prefer small, cohesive refinements over decorative additions.
